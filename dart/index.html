How it works

Three taps, one courier, no phone calls.

  1. Book

    Pick the pickup and drop-off, choose a scooter, car or van, and see the full price before you pay. Send now or schedule for later.

  2. Track

    The nearest courier accepts in seconds. Follow them live on the map, with routes planned around the ZTL and its hours.

  3. Delivered

    The courier takes a photo or a signature at the door. You get a notification, the proof and a receipt straight away.

Questions

Good to know

Where does DART deliver?

Everywhere inside the Grande Raccordo Anulare to start, with more of the province to follow. The covered area is drawn and changed from the admin panel.

How fast is it?

Most scooter jobs inside the centre arrive in under an hour. You see the expected arrival time before you book and live on the map after.

What can't I send?

Cash, medicines that need a prescription, alcohol to under-18s, weapons, and anything illegal. The full list is in the terms.

Can I schedule a delivery?

Yes. Choose a date and a time window when you book, up to 14 days ahead.

Is the app in Italian?

Yes. Both apps and the website will be in Italian and English.
