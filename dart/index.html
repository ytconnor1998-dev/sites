Before you ask

Where do you deliver?

Anywhere inside the Grande Raccordo Anulare. Fiumicino and the Castelli are next.

How fast is it?

Most scooter jobs in the centre arrive in under an hour. The app shows the arrival time before you book.

Can I book for later?

Yes, up to 14 days ahead. Pick a date and a one-hour window.

What can't you carry?

Cash, prescription medicines, weapons, and alcohol for anyone under 18. The full list is in our terms.
