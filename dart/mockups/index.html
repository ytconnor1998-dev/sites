DART · App mockupsTwo apps, one city

First-pass screens for the DART customer app and courier app (iOS and Android). The prices, names and addresses in Rome are examples, and the maps are placeholders for Google Maps.

Customer app

Post a delivery, compare bids, track it and chat. Light theme, cobalt accent.

Courier app

Find jobs, bid, run the route and get paid. Dark theme for riding at night, signal-yellow accent.
